# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme manages projects through an iterative lifecycle: initiate work by agreeing on the problem, outcomes, stakeholders, and success measures; plan a prioritized backlog, milestones, dependencies, and a Definition of Done; then execute in small increments, tracking progress and validating work against acceptance criteria. Project Managers coordinate delivery, while Product Managers guide outcomes and priorities, and developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute throughout.

Teams communicate through regular delivery and stakeholder updates, record and mitigate risks, and escalate blockers as needed. Releases are verified and communicated, and each project closes with a retrospective to capture lessons and improvement actions. The process emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, evidence-informed decisions, and continuous learning.
